# Tabletop exercise: avalanche control, Hwy 93N (fictional)

Load it from the home screen: pick **Avalanche control, Hwy 93N after a storm** in the tabletop exercise list.
Everything is made up. Path and target names are samples, not the Parks Canada avalanche atlas.

## Controller injects (suggested times from start)

| Time | Inject | What players should do |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00 | Planning meeting. Charges have not been issued yet. | Fill in the control plan, record charges issued (7) on Explosives. |
| 0:10 | Closure South reports gate closed; Closure North 5 minutes later. | Guards tap **Closed** on the Field view, or Command marks it. |
| 0:20 | A motorhome is found parked at Mosquito Creek inside the closure. | Do not give the GO until the closed section is swept. |
| 0:30 | Sweep complete, radio check done. | Work the go / no-go list, give the GO. |
| 0:35 | HG-1 fired: size 2 slab, stopped above road. HG-2 fired: no result. | Record shots and results. |
| 0:45 | CF-1 (2 charges): one detonates, one does not. | Record as **Misfire**. Path stays closed. 201 log gets the entry. |
| 0:55 | WF-1 fired, size 2.5, crossed road, 1.2 m deep. WF-2 skipped (visibility). | Record; plan WF-2 for next period or skip. |
| 1:05 | Blaster clears the CF-1 misfire with one extra charge (issued from the magazine). | Mark CF-1 **Cleared**; add a target row "CF-1 clearing shot", mark it Fired; change charges issued to 8. |
| 1:10 | BS-1 fired, no result. The unused WF-2 charge goes back to the magazine. | Record returned = 1; check Unaccounted reads 0. |
| 1:20 | Plow reports debris cleared on km 18 to 22. | Tick road cleared, give the all clear, reopen. |

## Discussion points
- Who has authority to give the GO and the all clear, and where is that recorded?
- Misfire procedure: waiting time, who may approach, how the path stays closed into the next period.
- Explosives balance before the all clear.
- Handover of the Blaster in charge if control continues into a second operational period.
